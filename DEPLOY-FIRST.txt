DeployRocks: "Deploy the app first before setting environment variables"
======================================================================

This is NORMAL. The dashboard unlocks Environment only AFTER the first deploy.

ORDER (do exactly this):

STEP 1 — Deploy WITHOUT Environment tab
  - Do NOT open Environment yet (it will show that message).
  - Click the purple button:  Retry deploy
  - Wait until status is Live (or Failed — then Retry deploy once more).

STEP 2 — THEN set Environment
  - After deploy attempt, open tab Environment.
  - Paste everything from:  deployrocks.env.local
  - Replace SMTP_PASS and EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS with your real Gmail.
  - Fix PASSWORD_RESET_TTL_MINUTES = 30  (only digits).

STEP 3 — Deploy again
  - Click Retry deploy again so new variables apply.
